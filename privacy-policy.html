Wardrobe AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use Wardrobe AI:

  • Your name (provided during onboarding)
  • Email address (if you create an account)
  • Photos you upload of your clothing items
  • Location data (used only for local weather — never stored on our servers)
  • Style preferences you select in the app

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AI-powered outfit suggestions and styling advice
  • To store your wardrobe items securely in your personal account
  • To show you weather-appropriate outfit recommendations
  • To improve the app experience

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase — for authentication and secure data storage
  • Anthropic Claude API — for AI styling conversations (your messages are processed but not stored by Anthropic)
  • RevenueCat — for managing subscriptions and payments

4. Your Photos

Photos you upload are stored securely in your private account. We do not share your photos with any third parties. You can delete your photos and account at any time from the app settings.

5. Data Security

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We use Firebase security rules to ensure only you can access your own data.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To delete your account and all associated data, go to Settings → Delete Account in the app, or contact us at the email below.

7. Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
