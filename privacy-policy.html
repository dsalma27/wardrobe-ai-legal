Wardrobe AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Wardrobe AI is a personal styling app that helps you organize your wardrobe and get outfit suggestions. We take your privacy seriously and only collect what we actually need to make the app work well for you.

1. What We Collect

When you use Wardrobe AI, we may collect:

  • Your name (entered during onboarding)
  • Email address (if you sign in with Google)
  • Photos you upload of your clothing items
  • Your location — only to fetch local weather for outfit suggestions, and never stored on our servers
  • Style preferences you choose inside the app

2. How We Use It

We use your information to:

  • Generate personalized outfit suggestions based on your wardrobe
  • Keep your clothing items saved securely in your account
  • Show weather-appropriate recommendations
  • Make the app faster and more reliable over time

We don't sell your data. We don't share it with advertisers. Simple as that.

3. Third-Party Services

To run the app, we use a few trusted third-party services:

  • Google Firebase — handles sign-in and stores your data securely
  • Anthropic Claude API — powers the AI styling conversations. Your messages are processed to generate suggestions but are not stored by Anthropic.
  • RevenueCat — manages your subscription and payment status

4. Your Photos

Photos you upload are stored in your private account and are only used to show your wardrobe inside the app. We never share your photos with anyone else. You can delete any photo — or your entire account — at any time from the app settings.

5. Data Security

Your data is encrypted both in transit and when stored. We use Firebase security rules so that only your account can access your own data. No one else can see your wardrobe.

6. Your Rights

You can access, update, or delete your personal data at any time. To remove your account and everything associated with it, go to Settings → Delete Account in the app. You can also reach us by email if you need help with anything.

7. Children

Wardrobe AI is not intended for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect any data from children under 13. If we find out we have, we'll delete it immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we make significant changes to this policy, we'll let you know inside the app before the changes take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the most recent version.
